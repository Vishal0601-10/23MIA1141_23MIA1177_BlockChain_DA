Synthetic workload · deterministic seeds · finite non-replenishable inventory · actual transaction commit required before a ticket becomes BOOKED. The run continues until inventory naturally sells out or all generated demand/retries are resolved. No artificial booking deadline is used. Inventory is never forced to zero; residual inventory is valid when demand is exhausted first.
